# WALL-E

**Genre:** Animated, Sci-fi | **Year:** 2008 | **Studio:** Pixar

## About
*WALL-E* is about a small robot who is left alone on Earth to clean up the trash. One day he meets a robot named EVE, and he follows her into space. I like it because it tells a big story with very few words.

> "I don't want to survive. I want to live!"

## Main Characters
- **WALL-E:** the little trash-cleaning robot
- **EVE:** the smart probe robot he falls for
- **M-O:** the tiny cleaning robot on the ship
- **Captain McCrea:** the captain of the Axiom

## Top 3 Favorite Moments
1. WALL-E meets EVE for the first time
2. The space dance with the fire extinguisher
3. The captain stands up from his chair
